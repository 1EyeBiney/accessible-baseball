
### v1.1.2 — Swing Styles & The Fat Pitch
- **Physics Calibration:** Integrated `swingStyle` modifiers. Choke Up adds a 15ms buffer to the timing window; Power Swing reduces it by 10ms but increases "Distance" telemetry.
- **Fat Pitch Mechanic:** Finalized the "Command Miss" logic. If the pitcher misses their target, the ball is routed to Zone 5. Any swing in the middle row (4, 5, 6) now results in a "Flush" hit on a command miss.
- **Audio Feedback:** Standardized `playPlateSync` volume to 0.5 to ensure it is audible over 100mph fastball Doppler effects.
- **Telemetry Buffering:** Added a `BASE.state.lastShot` object to store the precise timing offset and zone differential for post-pitch review.
### v1.1.1 — Hitting Logic Refinement
- **Input Guarding:** Fixed a bug where multiple swing audio cues could trigger during a single pitch; the first keypress now "locks" the swing.
- **Timing Calibration:** Adjusted the hitting window to be style-aware: Choke Up now provides a +/- 10ms wider window than Power swings.
- **System Key Pass-through:** Verified and reinforced that browser-level keys (F5, F11, etc.) bypass the game's `preventDefault()`.
- **Telemetry Update:** Added "Speed" and "Zone" output to the `#visual-buffer` for post-pitch debugging.
\### v1.1.0 — The 9-Zone Hitbox \& Swing Styles

\- \*\*Numpad Mapping:\*\* Implemented a 3x3 hitting grid using Numpad 1-9. Pressing a zone key now acts as the direct swing trigger.

\- \*\*Swing Style State:\*\* Added `standard`, `choke\_up`, and `power` styles, toggled via Numpad Plus and Minus.

\- \*\*Plate Crossing Cue:\*\* Introduced a high-frequency "sync pulse" when the ball reaches the hitting zone, occurring shortly before the catcher's mitt sound to assist with timing.

\- \*\*Contact Logic:\*\* Integrated basic "Thwack" audio feedback for successful timing (Standard Sine at 440Hz).

\- \*\*Keyboard Control:\*\* Reinforced strict focus management and `e.repeat` filtering, while preserving system keys (F5, F6, Ctrl+PgUp/Dn, Esc).
