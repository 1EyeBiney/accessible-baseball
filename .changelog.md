### v1.5.0 — The Cognitive Shift
- **Pitch Duration Locked:** All pitches now take exactly 750ms to cross the plate, removing velocity variation and shifting the focus entirely to pitch identification.
- **Acoustic Signatures Overhauled:** Replaced the LFO stutter system with distinct wave types for immediate vertical identification. High zones use a sharp `triangle` wave, Mid zones use a smooth `sine` wave, and Low zones use a gritty `sawtooth` wave.
- **"Ghost Swing" Lock-In:** Swapping mid-flight contact for delayed resolution. Pressing a swing key immediately plays a sharp "lock-in" UI tick, but the pitch audio continues its full flight.
- **Reaction Time Scoring:** The `offsetMs` timing system has been replaced with a "Reaction Time" calculation. Faster identification equals better contact. Swings locked in under 300ms result in Home Runs, under 450ms are Flush, under 600ms are Solid, and up to 750ms are Topped.
